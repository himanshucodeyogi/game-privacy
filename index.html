Privacy Policy

Princess Runner · Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Princess Runner (package name com.princessrunner.game), published by Himanshu Kashyap, handles information. It applies only to this game.

Summary

Princess Runner has no accounts, no ads and no analytics service. Gameplay, scores and purchases made with in-game currency are not sent to the developer or to any third party. Progress is stored on your device.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a profile in its private storage so it can remember your progress between sessions. The file is encrypted and never uploaded. It can include:

  • coins, keys, score, distance and unlocked characters
  • shop purchases and upgrade levels bought with in-game currency
  • settings such as sound
  • a device identifier and the time of the last save, written into that same file

A separate file on the device may record how a session was played — for example how long a run lasted and what the character ran into — along with the device model name and how much memory the phone has. That file also stays on the device. It is not uploaded.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, advertising ID, or payment details. The game has no user accounts and no login. There are no in-app purchases of real money and no advertisements.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share information with anyone. The game does not include advertising networks, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services, or social-network SDKs. The developer does not operate a server that receives data from the game.

Internet permission

The Android package declares the INTERNET permission because the Unity game engine adds it by default. Princess Runner does not use it to contact a server, load remote content, or send the information described above off your device.

Other device access

The game may vibrate the phone for feedback during play. It does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or storage outside its own private folder.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game does not transmit the information above, we do not receive personal information about the people who play it.

How to delete your data

Uninstalling Princess Runner deletes the saved profile and the local play log from your device. There is no account to close and no copy of this information kept by the developer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will replace this page and the date at the top will change.
